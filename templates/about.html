About Gifric Farm

Pioneering organic, sustainable poultry farming and fresh chicken supply in Hatfield, Harare.

Our Heritage

Raised with Care, Delivered Fresh

Founded with a deep commitment to sustainable agriculture, Gifric Farm is located at 84 St Andrews Rd, Hatfield, Harare. We supply high-quality organic poultry and fresh chicken at $7 per bird to households, restaurants, and retail partners.

We believe that premium chicken meat and eggs start with animal welfare and natural nutrition. Our free-range broilers and layers are reared in humane, open-air housing, fed 100% organic grain feeds formulated on our farm, and cared for by certified livestock specialists.
